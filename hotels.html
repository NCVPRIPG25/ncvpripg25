NEARBY HOTELS

Cygnett Park Asia in Jammu is located approximately 4.2 km from satwari Airport (Jammu Airport). This is beautifully styled hotel - strategically located close to the airport and the rich religious and picturesque sites in Jammu - offers spacious comfort along with Cygnett's unique, rich and hospitable Cygnetture service. It is a contemporary and stylish hotel that offers a comfortable stay for travellers visiting the city. Located in a prime area, this hotel provides convenient access to major attractions, business centers, and transportation hubs. The rooms are well-designed, spacious, and equipped with modern amenities for a pleasant stay. The hotel features a multi-cuisine restaurant serving a variety of dishes, a fitness center for guests to stay active, and a warm and attentive staff. With its convenient location, modern amenities, and warm hospitality, Cygnett Park Asia Jammu ensures a pleasant and memorable experience for guests visiting the city.


Lords Inn Jammu is a modern mid-range hotel located approximately 7.9 km from Satwari Airport, offering a comfortable and convenient stay in the heart of the city near the Jammu Bus Stand. The hotel features 33 well-furnished rooms, all equipped with essential amenities such as free Wi-Fi, tea/coffee makers, safes, and stylish interiors. With facilities like a fitness center, business services, banquet spaces, free parking, and airport transfers, Lords Inn caters well to both business and leisure travelers. Its central location, attentive service, and value-for-money offerings make it a dependable choice for visitors seeking comfort and accessibility in Jammu.


Radisson Blu Jammu is a luxurious 5-star hotel located approximately 7.1 km from Jammu’s Satwari Airport, offering a seamless blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Set along the Jammu–Srinagar Bypass, the hotel features 119 contemporary rooms and suites, equipped with modern amenities like high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, and plush interiors. With extensive event spaces, including a grand ballroom and lush lawns, the hotel is ideal for weddings, conferences, and social gatherings. Radisson Blu a preferred choice for both business and leisure travelers visiting Jammu.


Hotel Meridian Palace is a well-located mid-range hotel in the heart of Jammu, just 7 km from Satwari Airport, offering easy access to key transit points and city attractions. The hotel features a variety of spacious rooms and suites designed for comfort, with amenities such as free Wi-Fi, flat-screen TVs, minibars, 24-hour room service, and modern interiors. Guests can enjoy dining at the hotel’s rooftop restaurant, which offers a blend of Indian and Chinese cuisine, or unwind at the in-house bar. With well-equipped banquet and conference facilities that can accommodate large gatherings, the hotel is also a suitable venue for business events and social functions. Its central location, courteous staff, and practical offerings make Hotel Meridian Palace a reliable and affordable choice for both business and leisure travelers visiting Jammu.


Vivanta Jammu is a premium hotel strategically located just 5.6 km from Satwari Airport, offering quick access to Jammu’s commercial and cultural hubs. With contemporary architecture and sophisticated interiors, the hotel features 88 elegantly designed rooms and suites that offer views of the scenic Tawi River and historic Bahu Fort. The hotel also offers a fitness center, indoor pool, and spacious banquet halls ideal for business meetings and social events. Combining luxury with convenience, Vivanta Jammu is a perfect choice for both business travelers and tourists seeking comfort, style, and connectivity in the heart of the city.


Fortune Inn Riveria in Jammu is located approximately 6.2 km from satwari Airport (Jammu Airport). Fortune Inn Riviera, a member of ITC’s hotel group, is a premium business-class hotel located approximately 6.2 km from Jammu’s Satwari Airport. Overlooking the tranquil Tawi River and set against the backdrop of the majestic Pir Panjal mountains, the hotel offers a perfect blend of comfort and elegance. With well-appointed rooms and suites, modern amenities, and multiple dining options—including a multi-cuisine restaurant, a North Indian specialty eatery—it caters to both business and leisure travelers. Its convenient location near the city center, coupled with facilities like banquet halls, a fitness center, and travel assistance, makes it a preferred stay for visitors to Jammu.
